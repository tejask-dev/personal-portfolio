## Locked experience direction

Open Water is a sunlit field expedition: clear turquoise navigation channels, pale sand shelves, a visible reef, expressive wildlife, and an inhabited lighthouse. It should feel cinematic and playful, with crafted detail and strong environmental composition. Avoid generic luxury-yacht advertising, dark fog, empty scale, and random purchased assets with conflicting materials.

Dakota is a dolphin who identifies as a shark: confident, helpful, dryly funny. Use authored captions and expressive movement, with optional sound. No live chatbot or unsolicited monologue. The joke is Dakota's overconfidence, never an attack on real identities. Guidance is dismissible and never gates the work.

The expanded expedition has Crown Reef, Driftwall, Blue Cathedral and Lantern Grotto, with real reef relief and three-dimensional cave passages. Crown connects to Driftwall and Blue Cathedral; Blue Cathedral connects to Lantern. Every habitat is directly accessible from the chart. Preserve one original vessel return anchor across all travel and fish transformations. A crafted superyacht handles deliberately; the smaller tender owns races and ramps. Nemo and Dory are original clownfish and blue-tang art with visitor-triggered dialogue; controlling one transfers that existing character rather than spawning a duplicate. Catch-and-release fishing excludes named companions. A stable-view alternative must remain available. The lighthouse gives orientation and useful access to work. Professional content and contact are directly reachable without playing.

The audience is founders and hiring teams. The main outcome is contact about opportunities. Lead with Founding Software Engineer at Anticipation Labs, cross-system engineering ownership, and the three public-safe Anticipy lead stories. Account for all twelve contribution records while distinguishing deployed fixes, local validation, owner reports, and incomplete hardware readiness. Explain each page's purpose and show concrete evidence. Preserve the five existing case-study routes. Independent client work was removed at the owner’s request. Anticipy’s primary external destination is https://anticipy.ai/; Fellowship is coming soon and is not fully published. Distinguish Tejass's role from team/platform outcomes, and use honest status labels for current, past, and closed work.

## Research method

1. Inspect the actual current experience, implementation constraints, and approved public content. Describe concrete deficiencies and what to preserve.
2. Study primary creative references for composition, onboarding, movement, attention, discovery, character, and reading. Cite exact URLs. Extract principles; do not reproduce another designer's world.
3. Compare three materially different directions using matching scenes and explicit criteria: identity/proof clarity, distinctiveness, ecological coherence, interaction quality, mobile composition, accessibility, and implementation cost. Select one and explain the tradeoff.
4. Specify the opening, lighthouse, reef, and case-study transition on desktop and mobile. State framing, hierarchy, materials, palette, scale, timings, controls, and what the visitor understands.
5. Define encounters through different verbs and feedback, not twelve props opening the same panel. Include trigger, visitor action, response, repeat behavior, failure, keyboard/touch alternative, reduced motion, and silent use.
6. Challenge visual quality against real asset/animation pipelines, transfer and rendering budgets, legibility, and content evidence. Mark provisional budgets and unverified assets explicitly.
7. Return a decision-complete brief and critique. Cite factual source-derived claims near their use. Keep quotations brief; favor original synthesis.

## Required critique

For each finding state severity, visible problem, visitor consequence, exact proposed change, and an acceptance check. Separate release blockers from polish. Prioritize professional clarity and art consistency over feature count. Reject unsupported superlatives, fake traction counters, activity-to-impact metaphors, unreadable labels, mandatory audio, forced motion, and novelty controls for ordinary reading.

## Output contract

Return: recommendation; evidence and sources; selected/rejected directions; concrete scene and interaction decisions; professional-content mapping; mobile/accessibility/failure behavior; asset and performance risks; acceptance checks; remaining unknowns with a specific way to resolve them. If a source or provider is unavailable, report the limitation rather than silently substituting a claim.
