# Open Water

Read this and `tokens.css` before changing UI. The portfolio is an editorial
document with an optional ocean experience. Preserve its content and routes.

- Warm ivory, deep teal, Spectral display type and Aspekta text are the identity.
- Readability precedes spectacle: regular-weight body text, bounded measure,
  visible focus and ordinary links. Keep the strongest action visually distinct.
- Use the existing grid. Space separates ideas; borders group evidence.
- The illustrated boat-to-helm transition is the focal animation. Reading stays
  still. The underwater film is optional, pausable, muted and contained.
- Keep failure, no-JavaScript, reduced-motion and data-saving reading paths.
- New and revised shared UI values come from `tokens.css`. Do not tokenize
  unrelated scene geometry or rewrite the existing site solely to eliminate literals.
- No new copy, sections, dependencies, hover-lift cards, decorative shadows or
  repeated scroll reveals in a polish pass. Preserve the commissioned ocean art.

References and decisions live in `references/README.md`. Screenshot evidence and
subjective design scores live in `REVIEW.md`; a score is not accessibility proof.
Use `.claude/skills/design-loop/SKILL.md` for future focused passes.
