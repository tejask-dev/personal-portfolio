Open Water fish swimming adaptation

Source: 0s0ka/sea, src/fishMaterial.js
Commit: d773646bfe5e66d8ab312429591b4dbf12e3d389
https://github.com/0s0ka/sea/blob/d773646bfe5e66d8ab312429591b4dbf12e3d389/src/fishMaterial.js

Adapted the head-to-tail traveling wave for the existing original fish geometry.
Changes include an ascending normalized envelope, analytical deformed normals,
CPU companion deformation, per-instance school phases, speed/turn response and
Open Water's existing pause-aware simulation clock. No external models, water
renderer, application framework or dependency packages were imported.
