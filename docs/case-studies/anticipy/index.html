I am a Founding Software Engineer at Anticipation Labs, building Anticipy. It is an AI wearable being built to turn everyday conversations into actions through connected software, with user approval before execution.

The idea

A conversation can contain an intention worth following through on. Anticipy is being built around that gap: turn the intention into a proposed action, let the person approve it, and then carry it out through connected software.

Where I contribute

My contributions include the Fellowship website and application flows, deployment infrastructure, and early operations. That work sits between the product people encounter and the systems the team uses to deliver it.

I work as part of the Anticipation Labs team. The wearable and the wider product are team efforts; this page describes my engineering contribution.

A deliberate approval step

The product concept separates a suggested action from permission to execute it. User approval comes before the connected software action. The sequence above illustrates that distinction without representing a production interface.
