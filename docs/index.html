02 / A little about me

Curiosity,
put to work.

I like the stretch between an idea and something people can actually use—and the work it takes to close it.

I’m Tejass, a Founding Software Engineer at Anticipation Labs, building Anticipy. Previously, I was CTO at Stellar Learning.

I study Computer Science at Western University and hold Ivey AEO status. My work has taken me from application flows and backend systems to collaborative research.

I work with AI tools as part of the engineering process: set the problem, make the acceptance criteria concrete, review the result, and test what a person will actually experience. Sometimes that means a browser. Sometimes it means a phone and a prototype pendant on the bench.

I also love water, boats, and yachts. Open Water gives that curiosity a place in the portfolio. The setting is imagined; the work is real.
